The poolside chronicles

A SPLASH OF FUN AND AN UNWANTED PUBLICITY STUNT

One pool party, questionable DJ selections, two happy people, and an Instagram post that neither of them had signed up for.

A note from me to you

Remember this pool party? I remember the swimming, the splashing, the photographs, and you looking particularly good while diving into the pool. (Yes, I noticed. Obviously.) The only thing I would like to formally complain about is the music. I'm still not entirely sure what the DJ was playing, but apparently the entire playlist consisted of one continuous DHAM DHAM DHAM. A truly unforgettable contribution to the music industry. But we had fun anyway. And that's what mattered. Until the resort decided to make us the stars of its Instagram page. Now, I don't know what possessed them to publish our photograph without asking us, but suddenly we had a completely new activity for the day: getting that post taken down. The calls. The messages. The waiting. The repeated follow-ups. The sheer number of times we checked whether anyone had bothered responding. Hours went by, and it felt like we were conducting an entire diplomatic negotiation just to get one Instagram post deleted. And then, finally, SUCCESS. The post disappeared. Justice prevailed. Our little publicity crisis was over. I suppose most couples have their own stories about romantic getaways, lovely dates and memorable adventures. We, apparently, also have a story about jointly fighting a resort's social media department. Looking back, I find the whole thing rather funny. Not the unwanted post, obviously, but the ridiculous little adventure that followed. And despite the questionable soundtrack and the Instagram drama, I'd happily keep the memory of that day. Especially the part where it was just us, enjoying ourselves, completely in our own little world. Here's to more pool parties, better DJs, fewer uninvited Instagram appearances, and you continuing to look unfairly handsome while diving into pools. Some things are worth documenting. Some things, however, should stay in our camera roll.

A matter of public interest

A HAPPY MEAL, AN UNHAPPY LEVEL OF HANDSOMENESS

Local man visits McDonald's to order food. Girlfriend discovers yet another opportunity to photograph him looking ridiculously handsome.

A note from me to you

I would like to draw the court's attention to the following photograph. A perfectly ordinary day. A perfectly ordinary McDonald's. A man simply trying to order his food. And yet, somehow, we have a photograph worthy of publication. Look at you. Standing there, completely occupied with the important business of deciding what to eat, looking all thoughtful and composed, as if you're making a life-altering decision rather than ordering a burger. I don't know what it is about you, but even the most mundane activities become an opportunity for me to find you ridiculously attractive. Perhaps it's the glasses. Perhaps it's the hair. Perhaps it's the way you stand, or the little expressions you make when you're concentrating. Or perhaps I'm just hopelessly biased. Actually, let's not pretend otherwise. I am. I think one of the nicest things about being with someone is getting to see them in all these completely unremarkable moments. The restaurant visits, the food orders, the waiting around, the deciding what to eat, the little conversations that nobody else would think twice about. We don't need every day to be an adventure. Sometimes we can just get ourselves a meal, sit together, and enjoy being in each other's company. And sometimes, apparently, I can spend an entire food outing admiring you instead of concentrating on the menu. I hope you enjoyed your meal that day. I certainly enjoyed the view. P.S. I would like to formally request that you continue looking this handsome during all future food orders.

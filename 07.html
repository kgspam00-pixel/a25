The style desk presents

TRADITIONALLY HANDSOME, UNFAIRLY WELL-DRESSED

A kurta, a pair of Jordans, one very handsome man, and an office building that has no idea how lucky it is to have this particular view.

A note from me to you

Can we take a moment to appreciate this outfit? THE KURTA. THE JORDANS. I genuinely think this combination deserves its own fashion column. There's something about the way you carry yourself in traditional clothes, especially when you pair them with your sneakers. A little bit of old-school charm, a little bit of sneakerhead energy, and a whole lot of handsome. And here you are, standing outside our office building, looking like you have absolutely nowhere else to be and all the time in the world. I love this photograph because it's such an ordinary little moment. No elaborate date, no birthday celebration, no special occasion that we had to plan weeks in advance. Just you, dressed up, standing somewhere that's part of our everyday lives. And somehow, I still look at it and think, HOW IS THIS MAN SO HANDSOME? I think that's one of my favourite things about us. Even the most ordinary places and the most uneventful days end up having little moments worth remembering. Also, I hope you know that I am never going to stop appreciating your outfits. You can wear a kurta, a shirt, a sweatshirt, or something you've owned for ten years, and I will still have something to say about how good you look. And please, never stop wearing your Jordans. I rather like this particular combination of yours. To my handsome, well-dressed, slightly too-cool-for-his-own-good boy: I hope you keep giving me reasons to take photographs of you. Although, at this point, I suspect I don't need many reasons at all.

THE ART OF BEING UNREASONABLY HANDSOME

Caught completely unaware, lost in thought, adjusting his glasses. One photographer. One very distracted girlfriend.

A note from me to you

I don't think you even know I took this photograph. You were just sitting there, completely absorbed in your own little world, looking at something or thinking about something, adjusting your glasses, minding your own business. And there I was, behind the camera, absolutely losing it. LOOK AT YOU. I mean, where do I even begin? The hair, the glasses, that ridiculously handsome face, and those eyelashes. Those beautiful, unfairly long eyelashes. I don't think you understand how much I admire them. There's something about the way you look when you're deep in thought. So serious, so composed, so completely unaware of how good you look. You have this wonderfully intellectual, quietly handsome thing going on, and I don't think you even realise it. I love photographs like this because they don't need a pose, a smile, a special occasion or even your permission to become my favourites. You weren't looking at me. You weren't smiling for me. You weren't even aware that I was taking a picture. And somehow, I love it all the more. I hope you know that I find you attractive in all sorts of ways. When you're dressed up, when you're being ridiculous, when you're sleepy, when you're laughing, and apparently, even when you're just adjusting your glasses and thinking about the universe. You really are a very handsome man, Aman. And I'm afraid I'm going to keep taking photographs like these for as long as you'll let me. Consider this your official notice. With love, Your very biased photographer.

An accidental masterpiece

A LITTLE MISPRINT CALLED BLISS

It was supposed to say Ilios. The sketch had other plans. Perhaps it understood something we hadn't quite put into words.

A note from me to you

There are photographs you like, and then there are photographs you keep coming back to. This is one of mine. Remember Ilios? The name of the place was supposed to be written in the background, but when I turned our photograph into a sketch, the AI decided that it was going to be "Bliss" instead. I suppose I could have corrected it. But where's the fun in that? Because, honestly, with you, bliss doesn't always look like some grand, extraordinary thing. Sometimes it's just the two of us standing close, looking at each other, having a lovely time somewhere. I love the way we're looking at each other in this picture. I love the little details, the flowers, the background, and the fact that an accidental spelling mistake somehow made the entire picture more meaningful. Maybe the AI got one thing right without even trying. Ilios was the place. Bliss was the feeling. And if I get to keep finding bliss in all the places we go together, I think I'm doing pretty well. Here's to more places, more photographs, more accidental masterpieces, and all the little moments that turn out to mean a lot more than we expected.

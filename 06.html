An unexpected artistic revelation

THE NERD, THE PENCIL & THE GIRL HE DREW

Breaking news: Local nerd reveals secret artistic talent. Girlfriend left speechless. Pencil portrait becomes an instant treasured possession.

A note from me to you

I DID NOT KNOW THAT MY NERD COULD DRAW. I really, genuinely did not. And then, for my birthday, you casually revealed that you apparently have an entire artistic side that I had somehow remained completely unaware of. A HAND-DRAWN PENCIL PORTRAIT OF ME. You sat down, picked up a pencil, and made something with your own hands, something that was entirely yours to create and entirely mine to keep. I don't think you understand how much that means to me. There's something so special about being drawn by someone who loves you. Someone who has looked at your face enough times to recreate it on paper. Someone who notices you in ways you might not even notice yourself. And the best part? You surprised me. Completely. I remember holding that portrait and probably looking like I had just witnessed a miracle. Which, to be fair, I had. My very own boyfriend had secretly been hiding pencil skills from me all this time. I was so proud of you. So impressed. So ridiculously in love with the fact that you had made something so personal for me. I think that's what makes this gift different. It wasn't something you simply picked out or ordered. It was time, effort, patience and a little piece of your talent, all put together for me. And now I have a portrait drawn by you. I hope you know that I will treasure it for a very, very long time. Not just because it looks like me, but because of the person who drew it. Also, I would like to formally request more demonstrations of this previously undisclosed artistic talent. You cannot simply reveal that you're secretly an artist and then expect me to behave normally about it. And that is why you are being given these sketch sheets btw. My clever, talented, thoughtful boy. You really do keep finding new ways to surprise me. And I really do keep finding new reasons to fall a little more in love with you.

THE BIRTHDAY THAT GOT AN ENCORE

One week late, a little bit of devotion, lots of good food, and a photograph that found its way into your mum's favourites.

A note from me to you

My actual birthday had me occupied with college and convocation, so naturally, we decided that one celebration simply wasn't enough. A week later, we made a day of it. A visit to ISKCON, ramen together, and then getting dressed up and heading out in the evening to celebrate at the Big Tree. I love this photograph for so many reasons. I love how happy we look, how comfortably we're tucked into each other, and especially because I'm wearing that suit you absolutely adore. And apparently, you adore it enough to show this picture to your mum too. Which makes me love it just a little more. It's funny how some photographs become favourites without us even realising it at the time. This is one of those for me. My birthday might have been a week earlier, but this day was another little reminder that the best part of celebrating is having you beside me. Here's to all the birthdays, belated celebrations, random food dates, and photographs we'll collect along the way.

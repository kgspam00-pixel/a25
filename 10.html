A birthday wish, from my heart to yours

THE WORLD IS YOURS, MY LOVE. I HOPE IT TREATS YOU KINDLY.

Ten memories, countless photographs, a million little reasons to smile, and one very special person I get to celebrate today.

A letter for the birthday boy

And here we are. The tenth page. I suppose I could have filled this one with another funny story, another photograph, or another account of how ridiculously handsome I find you. But I think I'd rather use this space to tell you what I wish for you. First, I hope you're happy. Truly, deeply, peacefully happy. Not just on birthdays, not just when things are going your way, but even on the ordinary Tuesdays, the tiring days, and the days when life doesn't quite look the way you planned it. I hope you get to do all the things you've quietly dreamed of. I hope your hard work pays off, your plans fall into place, and the opportunities you deserve find their way to you. I hope you grow into everything you want to become, without ever feeling that you have to rush or prove yourself to the world. I hope you get to travel, explore, build things, achieve things, and experience all the wonderful little adventures life has waiting for you. I hope you always have reasons to laugh until your stomach hurts, people who genuinely show up for you, and a home that feels like your safest place. I hope the coming years are gentler, kinder and fuller of good surprises. I hope the things that have weighed on your mind become easier, the uncertainties become clearer, and the things you have been waiting for finally work out. And most importantly, I hope you never lose the parts of yourself that make you YOU. Your intelligence, your curiosity, your determination, your wonderfully stubborn ways, your sense of humour, your little quirks, and that soft side that I get to see in moments when you aren't even trying to show it. I hope you continue to discover new things about yourself. I hope you surprise yourself with what you're capable of. I hope you become prouder of the person you are and more excited about the person you're becoming. And I hope you know that you don't have to have everything figured out all the time. You are allowed to rest. You are allowed to change your mind. You are allowed to have difficult days. You are allowed to simply be. I hope you give yourself the same patience and kindness that you so often extend to the people you love. As for me, I hope I get to be around for many more of your birthdays. I want more photographs, more silly dates, more food adventures, more celebrations, more discoveries, more arguments about absolutely nothing, more inside jokes, and more ordinary days that somehow become my favourite memories. I want to see you achieve the things you work so hard for. I want to cheer for you, be proud of you, make you laugh, annoy you occasionally, and remind you that somebody out here thinks you're rather wonderful. I cannot promise that every year will be easy, or that every plan will work out exactly as we imagine. But I can wish you a life that is full of meaning, love, good health, opportunities, beautiful experiences and people who make it all worthwhile. Happy birthday, my love. May this new chapter bring you more happiness than you know what to do with, more success than you ever imagined, and more reasons to smile than I could possibly count. Here's to you, Aman. To everything you are, everything you are becoming, and everything that is still waiting for you. The world is yours. Go make something beautiful of it. And whenever you look back, I hope you find a thousand reasons to be proud of the life you've lived so far. I'm so glad I get to be a little part of it. With all my love, Your girl. ♥

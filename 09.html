A nostalgic night out

DORAEMON, DISAPPOINTMENT & A VERY GOOD SELFIE

Two grown-ups, one childhood favourite, a late-night movie that didn't quite deliver, and a selfie that absolutely did.

A note from me to you

Remember our late-night Doraemon movie date? Two perfectly grown adults deciding that the best way to spend an evening was to go back to our childhood and watch Doraemon on the big screen. I actually loved the idea. There is something rather special about revisiting the things that made us happy when we were younger, especially when you get to do it with someone you love. Unfortunately, the movie itself had other plans. I don't know what exactly was happening in that film. It was all over the place. We went in looking for nostalgia and came out with several unanswered questions. But you know what? I don't think I mind. Because I have this photograph. And I absolutely love this selfie. Look at us! The smiles, the closeness, the slightly silly, completely unplanned happiness. It feels so much like us. Not particularly posed, not trying to look perfect, just two people having a good time together. I think that's what I want to remember about that night. Not the plot, not the twists, not whatever was happening on that screen. Just us, staying out late, revisiting a little piece of childhood, and making another memory of our own. Maybe the movie didn't quite live up to our expectations, but the company certainly did. And if I had to choose between watching a perfectly written film alone and watching a completely confusing Doraemon movie with you, I think you already know which one I'd pick. Although next time, perhaps we should read the reviews before booking the tickets. Love, Your favourite fellow movie critic.
